GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

A few things to know about Kivo.

Who is Kivo for?

Kivo is being built for social sellers who want a more organised way to manage products, share a storefront, and keep track of orders.

Do my customers need a Kivo account?

The intended shopping experience supports guest checkout, so customers can shop without needing a seller account. The complete checkout flow must be implemented and tested before this is available in production.

Can I share my store on social media?

The storefront is designed to be shared through channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and TikTok. Availability depends on the relevant storefront features being implemented.

Can I change my plan later?

The plans are structured for different business sizes. Plan changes should be handled through the subscription and billing features once those flows are available.

Does Kivo receive my customers' sales payments?

Kivo's subscription fee is separate from the money you receive for your own sales. Seller payment processing needs to be configured and verified before accepting live payments.
